Data provenance and viewing details

The 24 forms and 172 actions come from the existing paired recording manifest. Each successful source observation contains 33 image points and 33 model-estimated world points. Only the current form's files are loaded. Display coordinates convert the model axes and use fixed source-facing rotations; they preserve subsequent turning and do not fuse the two recordings. Bone lengths and missing points are not replaced by a standard avatar. Wrist trails contain only recent, contiguous visible observations and clear when the playhead jumps. Joint angles are model-derived observations, with unavailable values left blank.
