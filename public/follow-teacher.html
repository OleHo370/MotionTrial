How the four-point game works

Match the same anatomical hand or foot to its color. Ring 1 is the start; ring 2 is the finish. A double ring marked H means hold. The brighter guides belong to this small step; other dots help you see the teacher's position.

The straight guides join observed start and end targets. They simplify the teacher's path; they do not claim that the real movement followed a straight line. Missing source targets are marked unavailable.

The back view is unmirrored. Front detail, camera dots and camera guides mirror with their respective videos. Joint identities never swap. Body anchors are used internally for size and position, but no full-body skeleton is drawn.

Stars record collected game targets, not Tai Chi correctness. One third of playable targets earns one star, two thirds earns two, and all earns three. Visual-only source steps are separate; live missing tracking never reduces the target count or earns stars. Game progress stays in this browser.
